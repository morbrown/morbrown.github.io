Research

Syntax, binding, agreement, and the relationship between grammatical structure and interpretation.

Research interests

My research explores questions in syntactic theory and its interfaces, especially the ways that morphosyntactic structure constrains interpretation. My work focuses primarily on Uyghur, alongside research on Finnish and Hindi-Urdu.

SyntaxBinding and anaphoraAgreement Turkic languagesNominalizationFinnish Hindi-Urdu

Current and recent projects

  • Binding and Agree in UyghurInvestigating long-distance reflexive binding and its implications for theories of binding and agreement.
  • Controllable pronouns in UyghurExamining the syntax and interpretation of pronouns whose interpretation depends on grammatical and discourse conditions.
  • Case change in Finnish embedded clausesRevisiting Finnish embedded clause patterns and their analysis in light of earlier work on Itkonen structures.
