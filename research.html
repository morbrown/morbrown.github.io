Experimental and corpus investigation of syntactic variation and change

I am also interested in how our theoretical commitments about grammatical structure interact with the empirical reality of synchronic variation and diachronic change, as well as how diverse methodologies, including experimental and corpus studies, can inform our understanding of those phenomena. In an ongoing project with Arto Anttila and Brandon Papineau, we probe variation in nominal case in a set of embedded environments in contemporary Finnish, and are able to demonstrate that it is the consequence of a change in progress. We are currently assembling our findings in a full-length manuscript, but until then you can read more about this project here and here.
